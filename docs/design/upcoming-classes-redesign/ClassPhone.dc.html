About this class

In this 6-class studio series, you will have enough time to deeply learn and become comfortable making stained glass, with lots of time for personalized instruction.

You can design your own pattern or choose from ours, including ones larger and more complex than a TryIt or Workshop class. Choose your glass colors, score and cut glass, wrap in copper foil, and solder with our lead-free tin solder.

What's included

Colored glass Copper tape Lead-free solder Hanging wire

What to bring

Closed-toed shoes. No food or open cups in the studio.
