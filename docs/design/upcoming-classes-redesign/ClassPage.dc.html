About this class

In this 6-class studio series, you will have enough time to deeply learn and become comfortable making stained glass. You will have lots of time for personalized instruction, and can also design your own pattern if you would like.

Choose anything you'd like from our patterns, including patterns that are more complex and larger than what you can make in a TryIt or Workshop class. Choose from our selection of glass colors, score and cut glass, wrap in copper foil, and solder using our lead-free tin solder.

What's included

  • Colored glass
  • Copper tape
  • Lead-free solder
  • Soldering chemicals
  • Hanging wire

What to bring

Closed-toed shoes. Please don't bring food or open cups into the studio.
